Why the Humble Potato?

From the Andes to your dinner plate, the potato has a rich history. It’s a staple food globally, providing sustenance and flavor in countless forms: mashed, fried, baked, roasted, and more. Potatoes aren't just delicious; they are also a good source of vitamins, minerals, and complex carbohydrates.

Popular Potato Varieties

Russet

Classic baking potato. Floury and light when cooked, perfect for baking and fluffy fries.

Yukon Gold

All-purpose potato. Buttery flavor and smooth texture. Great for mashing, boiling, and roasting.

Red Bliss

Waxy texture. Holds shape well when cooked. Ideal for boiling, steaming, and potato salads.

Fingerling

Small and firm. Unique, often nutty flavor. Beautiful roasted whole or used in salads.

Featured Potato Recipe: Classic Creamy Mashed

Ingredients: 2 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes, 1/2 cup butter, 1/2 cup heavy cream (or milk), Salt & Pepper to taste.

Instructions: Peel and cube potatoes. Boil until fork-tender (15-20 mins). Drain well. Mash the potatoes while hot. Stir in butter and cream. Season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately and enjoy!
